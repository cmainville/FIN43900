# Cathie Wood SEC analysis

Run `../warren-buffett-sec/scripts/sec_analysis.py TICKER` with `SEC_CONTACT_EMAIL` set. The shared Python script retrieves five fiscal years of SEC 10-K facts and calculates revenue CAGR and latest R&D/revenue. The default thresholds are 15% and 5%. Missing R&D means **Not Known**, never zero. Use only printed numbers in a short, clearly labeled Wood-style simulation memo. Discuss growth and research investment, while noting that these checks cannot prove disruption or future returns. Do not calculate new figures or assert a technology moat from these facts alone.

The investing lens is adapted from [the Cathie Wood expert reference](https://github.com/CinderZhang/FAskills/blob/main/financial-researcher/experts/cathie_wood.md). Its broader forward-looking claims require evidence outside this lab's 10-K fact panel.
