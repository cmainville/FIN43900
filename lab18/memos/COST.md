# Costco — Buffett-style investment memo (simulation)

**Recommendation: Watch**

I can understand Costco's membership warehouse business, and its scale and customer loyalty may form a durable advantage. The figures alone cannot prove the moat or tell me whether management allocates capital well.

The reported record is strong. Revenue rose from $226,954 million to $303,154 million, while net income rose from $5,844 million to $9,226 million. ROE was at least 25.1% in every year shown, and the Python ROE check passed. Owner earnings were positive in every year and increased from $3,501 million to $9,382 million, so both owner earnings checks passed. That combination suggests consistent profitability and cash generation.

I would keep this business on my watch list while investigating the durability of its advantage, management, and the cash demands of future growth.
