# Simulation: Michael Burry-style investment memo — COST

**Verdict: Own**

Buffett’s lens would emphasize durable returns and owner earnings; Wood’s would test revenue growth and R&D commitment. This simulation uses Burry’s lens: cash conversion and balance-sheet resilience.

Operating cash flow exceeded net income in all five years. Owner earnings were positive throughout and rose from $3,501 million in 2022 to $9,382 million in 2026. The liabilities-to-equity ratio fell from 2.11x to 1.49x. Those figures support **Own**.

The case has limits. R&D figures were not found, and the output provides no cash balance, debt maturities, or liability breakdown to test resilience under stress.

I trust Burry's verdict to an extent because Costco consistently generates operating cash flow above net income and has a manageable liabilities-to-equity ratio, but I would want to further evaluate its debt obligations and overall financial risks before investing.
