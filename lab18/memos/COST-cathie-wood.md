# COST investment memo — Cathie Wood-style simulation

**Verdict: Pass.** Revenue rose from $226,954 million in 2022 to $303,154 million in 2026, but the reported 7.51% revenue CAGR falls short of the 15% growth threshold in the supplied output. R&D figures were not found, so this simulation cannot assess the other central part of Wood’s lens: commitment to research and development.

The figures speak more clearly to the other lenses. ROE stayed above 15% and owner earnings were positive in every year, which supports Buffett’s focus on durable returns and owner earnings. Operating cash flow exceeded net income in every year, and the latest liabilities-to-equity ratio was 1.49x, which supports Burry’s focus on cash conversion and balance-sheet resilience. Neither observation resolves the missing R&D evidence for a Wood-style decision.

I trust Wood's verdict to an extent because Costco falls short of her 15% revenue growth threshold, but the missing R&D data makes it difficult to fully assess the company using her investment strategy.
